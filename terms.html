What the Service Does

The Service allows users to:

Submit a CV and job description for AI-powered analysis of potential reasons for rejection
Optionally submit their LinkedIn profile text for comparative analysis
Optionally submit their analysis to a queue for review and written feedback from human HR professionals
Retrieve HR feedback using a unique submission ID

The AI analysis is powered by Anthropic's Claude API. HR reviewer submissions are stored in a Supabase database. By using the Service, you also accept the applicable terms of these third-party providers.

04

Data, Privacy & Storage

Standard analysis (no HR submission): Your CV text and job description are transmitted to the Anthropic Claude API for processing and are not stored by this Service beyond the duration of your session. Anthropic's own data retention policies apply.

HR review submissions: If you opt in to HR review, the following data is stored in our Supabase database:

Your name or alias (as you provide it — you may use a pseudonym)
An excerpt of your CV (first 600 characters)
An excerpt of the job description (first 400 characters)
The full AI analysis result in JSON format
HR reviewer feedback, reviewer name, and timestamp — once feedback is submitted

Do not include sensitive personal data in your submission. This includes national identification numbers, passport numbers, financial account details, passwords, medical information, or any data you would not want stored in a third-party database. The Service is not designed to handle sensitive personal data and accepts no liability for such data submitted by users.

You may request deletion of your submission at any time by contacting peter.jankovic@hotmail.sk with your submission ID. Requests will be processed within 30 days.
