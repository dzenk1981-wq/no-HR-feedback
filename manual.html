Overview

What this tool does

You paste your CV and a job description. The tool tells you — honestly — why you were probably rejected. No sugarcoating. No generic advice. You can also get a real human HR professional to review your application and leave written feedback.
🔍
CV Analysis
AI compares your CV against the job description and returns an ATS score, keyword gaps, and ranked rejection reasons.
💼
LinkedIn Review
Paste your LinkedIn profile alongside your CV. The tool checks consistency and flags what's missing from your profile.
👥
HR Feedback
Submit anonymously for a real HR professional to read your application and leave written feedback.
📊
HR Dashboard
HR professionals access a private dashboard to review submissions and write feedback for candidates.
ℹ️
No account needed. You can run a full CV analysis without signing up for anything. Only HR reviewers need an access code.

Feature 1

CV & Job Description Analysis

The core feature. Paste your CV and the job description you applied for and get an honest AI analysis of why you were likely rejected.
1
Open the app and stay on the Candidate tab
When the app loads you will see three tabs at the top: Candidate, Check HR Feedback, and HR Reviewer. You are already on the right one — Candidate.
2
Add your CV — upload or paste
You have two options:

Option A — Upload a file: Click the upload box on the left side. Select your CV file. Supported formats: .pdf .docx .txt. Your filename appears in green when uploaded successfully.

Option B — Paste text: Click inside the text area below the upload box. Copy your CV from Word or any other document and paste it directly.
💡
Tip: Pasting text gives the most accurate results. If uploading a PDF, the tool reads it as plain text — make sure your CV is not a scanned image.
3
Paste the job description
On the right side you will see a large text area labelled Job Description. Go to the job posting — on LinkedIn, a company website, or any job board — select all the text and paste it here. Include everything: the intro, responsibilities, requirements, and nice-to-haves. More text gives better results.
⚠️
Important: Do not paste a job title only. The tool needs the full description to analyse keyword gaps accurately.
4
Click "Analyse my application →"
The yellow button at the bottom. The tool will show a loading screen with status messages while it processes. This takes 10–20 seconds. Do not close the browser tab during this time.
5
Read your results
Your results appear on the same page. You will see your ATS score, the most likely rejection reasons ranked 1–4, keyword gaps, and five specific things to fix. See the Reading your results section below for a full explanation of each part.
6
Analyse another application
Scroll to the bottom of your results and click "← Analyse another application". All fields are cleared and you can start again with a new CV or job description.

Feature 2

LinkedIn Profile Review

Activate this alongside your CV analysis to check whether your LinkedIn profile is consistent with your CV and optimised for the role you applied for.
1
Complete steps 1–3 of the CV Analysis first
Add your CV and job description before activating LinkedIn review. The LinkedIn analysis runs alongside the CV analysis — not separately.
2
Turn on the LinkedIn toggle
Below the job description box you will see a toggle switch labelled "Also review my LinkedIn profile". Click it to switch it on. A blue section will appear below with instructions and two fields.
3
Copy your LinkedIn profile text
Go to your LinkedIn profile. Copy the text in one of these ways:

Option A — Save as PDF: On your LinkedIn profile click More → Save to PDF. Open the PDF, select all text (Ctrl+A or Cmd+A), copy and paste into the text area.

Option B — Manual copy: On your LinkedIn profile, copy your headline, your About section, and each experience entry one by one. Paste them all into the text area.
💡
Tip: The more of your LinkedIn profile you paste, the more accurate the comparison. At minimum include your headline, About section, and your two most recent roles.
4
Optionally paste your LinkedIn URL
The URL field above the text area is optional. You can paste your LinkedIn URL there (e.g. linkedin.com/in/yourname) for reference — it is included in your results but not used for analysis.
5
Click "Analyse my application →"
The analysis now runs on both your CV and your LinkedIn profile together. Your results will include a second score specifically for LinkedIn, a comparison between your CV and profile, LinkedIn-specific keyword gaps, and LinkedIn strengths.

Feature 3

Submit for HR Professional Feedback

After the AI analysis, you can also have a real human HR professional review your application and leave written feedback. This is optional and anonymous.
1
Complete steps 1–3 of the CV Analysis first
Add your CV and job description before enabling HR submission. The HR submission happens at the same time as the analysis — not separately.
2
Turn on the HR feedback toggle
Below the LinkedIn toggle you will see a second toggle labelled "Submit for HR professional feedback". Click it to switch it on. A field will appear asking for your name or alias.
3
Enter your name or alias
Type your name, initials, or any alias you choose. This is shown to the HR reviewer so they know who they are giving feedback to. You can stay fully anonymous by typing something like "Anonymous" or "Candidate A".
ℹ️
What is stored: Your name/alias, the first 600 characters of your CV, the first 400 characters of the job description, and the AI analysis result. Your full CV is not stored.
4
Click "Analyse my application →"
The analysis runs and your submission is saved to the HR reviewer queue at the same time. This adds 1–2 seconds to the loading time.
5
Save your Submission ID
After your results appear, a green banner will show your unique Submission ID — for example sub_4f7a2c1b. Copy this and save it somewhere safe. You will need it to check for HR feedback. It is not stored anywhere else and cannot be recovered if lost.
⚠️
Important: If you lose your Submission ID you cannot retrieve your HR feedback. Save it in your notes, email it to yourself, or screenshot it immediately.
📬
Feature 4

Checking for HR Feedback

Once you have submitted for HR review and saved your Submission ID, you can check back at any time to see if a reviewer has left feedback.
1
Click the "Check HR Feedback" tab
At the top of the app you will see three tabs. Click Check HR Feedback — the middle tab with a green dot.
2
Enter your Submission ID
Type or paste your Submission ID into the field. It looks like sub_4f7a2c1b. It is case-sensitive — copy it exactly as it appeared in your results.
3
Click "Check for feedback →"
Two outcomes are possible:

Feedback received: A green box appears showing the HR reviewer's written feedback, their name, and the date they responded.

Pending: An orange box appears telling you your submission is in the queue but no feedback has been left yet. Check back in 24–48 hours.
4
Check back as many times as you need
There is no limit on how many times you can check. Your Submission ID remains valid indefinitely. HR reviewers are volunteers — response times vary, but most submissions receive feedback within 48 hours.

Feature 5

HR Reviewer Dashboard

For HR professionals, recruiters, and hiring managers who have been invited to review candidate submissions. You need an access code to enter.
1
Click the "HR Reviewer" tab
At the top of the app click the HR Reviewer tab — the rightmost tab with a yellow dot. You will see the login form.
2
Enter your name
Type your name in the first field. This is shown to candidates when they receive your feedback — for example "Sarah — Senior Recruiter" or simply your first name. Be as specific or as anonymous as you prefer.
3
Enter the access code and log in
Enter the access code provided to you by the tool administrator. Click "Enter dashboard →" or press Enter. If the code is correct you will be taken to the submission dashboard immediately.
ℹ️
If you need the access code and have not received it, contact peter.jankovic@hotmail.sk
4
Browse submissions
The dashboard shows all candidate submissions sorted by date — newest first. Each card shows the candidate's name, submission date, their unique ID, ATS score, and a short excerpt of the AI verdict. You can see at a glance which submissions already have feedback and which are waiting.
5
Click "Review & give feedback" on any submission
A panel opens showing the full AI verdict, top rejection reasons, CV excerpt, and job description excerpt. Read through everything before writing your feedback.
6
Write your feedback
Type your feedback in the text area at the bottom of the panel. There is no character limit. Be honest and constructive — candidates are looking for real professional perspective, not encouragement. Address the specific gaps the AI identified and add anything you notice that the AI may have missed.
⚠️
Reviewer guidelines: Feedback must be professional and constructive. Do not contact candidates directly. Do not use submission data for recruitment outreach. See the Terms of Service for full reviewer obligations.
7
Click "Save feedback →"
Your feedback is saved instantly to the database. The candidate can retrieve it immediately using their Submission ID. The submission card on the dashboard updates to show "✓ Feedback given". You can edit your feedback at any time by reopening the submission.
8
Refresh and sign out
Click ↻ Refresh at any time to load new submissions that arrived since you logged in. When finished click Sign out to return to the login screen.

Reference

Reading your results

Here is what every part of your analysis means.
ATS Score
A percentage showing how well your CV matches the job description's keywords. Under 55% — likely filtered before a human read it. 55–74% — borderline, a recruiter decided. 75%+ — strong match, rejection was likely situational.
Rejection Stage
Where in the hiring process you were most likely stopped. ATS filter means no human saw your CV. Recruiter review means a human screened you out. Hiring manager means you got further but were not selected at final stage.
Overall Verdict
2–3 sentences summarising the most likely reason for rejection. This is intentionally direct — it is the honest assessment a recruiter would give if they were speaking frankly.
Rejection Reasons 1–4
The most likely reasons ranked by probability. Reason 1 is the most critical — fix this first. Each reason includes a short explanation of why it caused the rejection.
Keywords Missing
Red tags — words and phrases in the job description that do not appear anywhere in your CV. ATS systems scan for these. Add them where genuinely applicable.
Present but Weak
Orange tags — keywords that exist in your CV but are buried, mentioned only once, or not prominent enough to score well in ATS ranking.
Keywords You Have
Green tags — keywords from the job description that are already well represented in your CV. These are your strengths for this role.
Profile Mismatch
Appears only when the fundamental issue is not keywords but a deeper gap — wrong industry background, missing years of experience, or a required qualification you do not have. This is the most important section to read if it appears.
What to Fix
Five specific, actionable recommendations. These are ranked by impact — start with number one. Each recommendation is tailored to your specific CV and job description, not generic advice.
LinkedIn Score
Appears only when LinkedIn review is enabled. A separate score for your LinkedIn profile against the same job description. Below the score you will see what is inconsistent between your CV and LinkedIn, gaps on your profile, and LinkedIn-specific strengths.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is my CV stored anywhere?
Only if you opt in to HR review. Standard analysis sends your CV to the Claude AI API for processing — it is not stored by this tool after the analysis is complete. If you submit for HR review, the first 600 characters of your CV are stored in our database alongside the AI analysis result.
How accurate is the AI analysis?
It is a well-informed estimate, not a guarantee. The tool analyses keyword matching, profile fit, and common ATS filtering patterns. It cannot know the specific criteria a particular company uses or whether the role was filled internally. Treat it as honest guidance, not a definitive verdict.
How long does HR feedback take?
HR reviewers are professionals who contribute voluntarily. Most submissions receive feedback within 24–48 hours. There is no guaranteed response time. If your submission has been waiting more than 5 days without feedback, contact peter.jankovic@hotmail.sk with your Submission ID.
I lost my Submission ID. Can I get it back?
Unfortunately no — Submission IDs are not linked to any personal account or email address by design, for privacy reasons. If you lose it, submit again for a new analysis. In future, save your ID by emailing it to yourself immediately after receiving it.
Can I request deletion of my submission?
Yes. Email peter.jankovic@hotmail.sk with your Submission ID and a deletion request. Your submission will be removed within 30 days in compliance with GDPR.
How do I become an HR reviewer?
Contact peter.jankovic@hotmail.sk with a brief description of your HR or recruitment background. Verified HR professionals, recruiters, and hiring managers are welcome. The access code is shared only with verified reviewers.
Can I use this tool multiple times?
Yes — as many times as you like, for as many different job applications as you want. Each analysis is independent. Click "← Analyse another application" after each result to start fresh.
Does the tool work for any industry or role type?
Yes. The analysis is based on the content of your specific CV and job description — it is not limited to any industry. It works for tech, finance, NGO, operations, marketing, and any other field as long as you provide a full job description.
